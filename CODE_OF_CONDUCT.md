# Code of conduct

Identizen is a small open-source project about trust. We expect the same of the people in it.

## Expected

- Be direct and be kind. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Ask before concluding.
- Keep discussions on topic and in the open. Decisions about the project happen in issues and pull requests, where everyone can read them.
- Respect people's time. Come with a reproduction, a proposal, or a question that has been searched for first.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind, in any project space.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Anything that would be unacceptable in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainers through the [contact form](https://identizen.com/contact/) and say it concerns conduct. Reports are read by the maintainers only and handled privately. Outcomes range from a conversation to removal from the project's spaces, at the maintainers' judgement.

This applies to every space the project runs: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any chat or event held under the project's name.
